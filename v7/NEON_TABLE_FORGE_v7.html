Nudge rotates the selected block by one cell. Push moves a single cell through its path; intervening contents slide into the space it leaves.

Select mode: drag across cells to make a range. Move mode: drag a selected cell or range to swap it with another region. Copy mode duplicates the selected region. Push mode moves one cell through a row or column, shifting the intervening contents. Drag the small grips in row/column headers to reorder them. Alt+Arrow nudges the selection by one cell. Double-click nested tables to edit them in this same studio.
